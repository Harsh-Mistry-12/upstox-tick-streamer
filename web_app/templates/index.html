📚 Option Greeks — Quick Reference Hover columns in the Live Chain table for tooltips
Greek Symbol What it means Typical Range Interpretation
Delta Δ How much the option price moves when the underlying moves ₹1 Call: 0 → 1
Put: -1 → 0		 ATM ≈ 0.5. Higher delta = more sensitive to spot.
Gamma Γ Rate of change of Delta per ₹1 move in underlying Always positive; peaks at ATM High Gamma near expiry = rapid Delta swings. Risky.
Theta Θ Daily time decay — how much the option loses per day (in ₹) Always negative Higher absolute value = faster decay. Sellers profit from Theta.
Vega V Option price change per 1% increase in Implied Volatility Always positive High Vega = sensitive to IV changes. Peaks near ATM.
IV — Implied Volatility — the market's expectation of future price range % annualised (e.g. 14%) High IV → expensive options. Compare call IV vs put IV for skew.
PoP% — Probability of Profit — statistical chance the option expires in-the-money 0–100% ATM ≈ 50%. Deep ITM call ≈ 80–90%. Sellers aim for <30% PoP.
PCR — Put-Call Ratio — Total Put OI divided by Total Call OI >1 bearish · <1 bullish PCR > 1.2 often signals oversold (contrarian bullish). < 0.7 = bearish.
ITM Call — In-the-Money call — Strike < Spot price — Has intrinsic value. Green rows in table.
ATM ★ — At-the-Money — Strike nearest to current Spot — Highest Gamma & Vega. Amber row in table.
ITM Put — In-the-Money put — Strike > Spot price — Has intrinsic value. Rose rows in table.
